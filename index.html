About Arkaan AI Center

Catalyzing Somalia’s AI Revolution from Mogadishu

Arkaan AI Center is an advanced innovation, training, and research hub established at Wadajir, KM4, Mogadishu. We are dedicated to accelerating technological self-reliance across East Africa.

By integrating foundational theory, state-of-the-art robotics labs, and real industry capstones, we equip Somali youth, researchers, and public institutions with high-demand digital capabilities.
