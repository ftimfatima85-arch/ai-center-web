Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to questions regarding our programs, prerequisites, and registration.

Arkaan AI Center is an advanced education and innovation hub located in Mogadishu, Somalia. We provide practical, mentor-led programs in artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotics, and software engineering.
We offer comprehensive courses including AI Fundamentals, Machine Learning Mastery, Data Analytics & Business Intelligence, Full-Stack AI Web Apps, and Autonomous Robotics.
You can register directly on our website by visiting the Registration Page, selecting your preferred program, and submitting your details. Our academic team will contact you within 24–48 hours.
Our physical innovation facility is located at Wadajir, KM4, Mogadishu, Somalia. Visitors are welcome during regular campus operating hours.
Our programs cater to motivated university students, high school graduates, software developers, and working professionals seeking to transition into the artificial intelligence and data industry.
