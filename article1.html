Arkaan AI Center Launches New AI Fundamentals Program for 2026

📅 November 15, 2026 • 👤 Academic Department • ⏱️ 4 min read

Mogadishu, Somalia — Arkaan AI Center has officially announced the launch of its updated AI Fundamentals & Applied Machine Learning curriculum, tailored specifically to empower university students, young developers, and tech professionals with practical hands-on experience.

Empowering the Next Generation of Somali Technologists

The comprehensive 8-week program covers core computational reasoning, linear algebra for deep learning, Python programming, neural networks, and prompt engineering. Students will spend over 60% of their curriculum hours in interactive laboratory sessions developing functional models rather than purely memorizing theory.

"Our goal is not just teaching syntax; it is preparing innovators who can apply AI models to solve real challenges in healthcare, logistics, and education across East Africa."
— Eng. Faduma Omar Jeilani, Program Coordinator

Curriculum Highlights

  • Foundations of Intelligent Systems: Logic, search algorithms, and machine cognition.
  • Data Preprocessing & Visualization: Practical mastery with Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib.
  • Applied Machine Learning: Supervised and unsupervised learning with Scikit-learn.
  • Introduction to Deep Learning: Convolutional networks and Transformers with PyTorch.
  • Capstone Project: Building and deploying a live, production-grade AI solution.

Registration and Enrollment

Applications are now open for the upcoming cohort commencing next month. Prospective candidates can review prerequisites and submit applications via our online portal.
