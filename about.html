WHO WE ARE

Pioneering AI & Innovation in Somalia

Arkaan AI Center is a center of excellence established to foster talent, strengthen public and private institutions, and accelerate digital transformation.

Our Mission

Bridging Intelligence.
Building What Comes Next.

Arkaan AI Center operates at the intersection of emerging technologies, youth capacity development, and strategic institutional advisory. Located in Wadajir, KM4, Mogadishu, our center serves as a collaborative hub connecting students, engineers, university faculties, and government leaders.

We believe that local talent, when supported with world-class computational resources, hands-on mentorship, and ethical leadership training, will solve East Africa's most pressing socio-economic challenges.

Strategic Foundations

Our Three Pillars of Excellence

A balanced model ensuring technical mastery, societal relevance, and sustainable career pipelines.

1. Learn & Master

Rigorous technical foundations in artificial intelligence, neural networks, computer vision, and cloud engineering taught by industry practitioners.

2. Policy & Governance

Working directly with national ministries and development agencies to build resilient digital frameworks, ethics protocols, and data sovereignty.

3. Build & Deploy

Transitioning from theory to production by launching student startups, enterprise capstone prototypes, and applied AI systems across Somalia.

Empowering AI Leadership. Building Somalia's Digital Future. Bridging human talent with intelligent technology across the Horn of Africa.
