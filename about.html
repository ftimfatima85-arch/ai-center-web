About Arkaan AI Center

Mogadishu's leading innovation and AI education hub — shaping the next generation of technology leaders.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, Arkaan AI Center was established with a bold mission: to bridge the technology gap in Somalia and empower young Somalis with the skills of the future.

Nestled in the heart of Mogadishu at Wadajir, KM4, our center has grown into a vibrant ecosystem where students learn, build, and innovate. From humble beginnings, we have already trained hundreds of students across multiple AI and technology disciplines.

We believe that Africa's future is digital — and Somalia's youth will lead that transformation. Arkaan AI Center is the catalyst.
