About Arkaan AI Center

Pioneering practical artificial intelligence education, autonomous robotics research, and innovation in Mogadishu, Somalia.

Institutional Background

Our Inception & Mandate

Established in 2024 at Wadajir, KM4, Mogadishu, Arkaan AI Center was founded with a transformative objective: to bridge the vast gap between high-level theoretical academics and industry-ready artificial intelligence deployment in East Africa.

Recognizing that Somalia’s youthful demographic represents its most potent natural advantage, we established dedicated computing clusters, robotics testbeds, and an intensive mentorship ecosystem that rivals international technology institutes.

Strategic Direction

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Guiding principles shaping our technical rigor and civic commitment.

Our Mission

To democratize access to advanced artificial intelligence competencies, nurture indigenous technological solutions, and drive sustainable socio-economic growth in Somalia.

Our Vision

To stand as East Africa’s preeminent center of excellence for artificial intelligence innovation, recognized globally for groundbreaking research and elite engineering talent.

Strategic Goals

Train 2,500 certified engineers by 2028, establish 10 corporate joint venture labs, and incubate Somali-language multimodal foundation models.
