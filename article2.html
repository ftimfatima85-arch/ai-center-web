Arkaan Student Robotics Team Secures First Place in National AI Contest

📅 November 8, 2026 • 👤 Robotics Lab • ⏱️ 3 min read

A multidisciplinary engineering team from Arkaan AI Center has been awarded first place in the annual National Innovation Challenge for their Autonomous Obstacle-Evading Drone System designed for precision search-and-rescue.

Engineering Under Constraint

The drone prototype was completely developed inside the Arkaan Robotics Lab using embedded Raspberry Pi compute modules, edge-optimized MobileNet neural vision pipelines, and custom LiDAR sensor integration. Operating in GPS-denied indoor environments, the drone navigated a randomized obstacle course in record time.

"Our students designed inverse kinematics algorithms and computer vision pipelines from scratch. Seeing their solution operate with zero collision error on stage proved the strength of practical training."
— Lead Robotics Mentor, Arkaan AI Center

Project Technical Architecture

  • On-Board Vision: Stereo camera stream processed at 30 FPS using edge quantization.
  • Path Optimization: Reinforcement learning algorithm trained in simulated virtual environments.
  • Fail-Safe Telemetry: Low-power RF telemetry link streaming real-time diagnostic graphs.
