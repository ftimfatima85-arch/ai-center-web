Arkaan AI Center Inks Multi-Institutional Partnership to Train 1,000+ Students in Applied AI

📅 October 28, 2026 • 👤 Executive Directorate • ⏱️ 5 min read

In a landmark move to accelerate technological workforce readiness, Arkaan AI Center has formalized collaborative memorandums of understanding with regional universities and technical colleges across Mogadishu.

Joint Certification & Lab Sharing

Under the partnership framework, undergraduate computer science, software engineering, and statistics students will gain direct access to Arkaan’s GPU-accelerated computing clusters, specialized robotics hardware, and industry-grade mentorship.

The initiative includes credit-transferable elective modules, joint hackathons, sponsored graduation projects, and direct internship pipelines into local enterprise partners.

"By joining forces with academic institutions, we are establishing a continuous talent bridge that moves students directly from foundational classroom theory into high-impact AI engineering careers."

Core Partnership Initiatives

  • Open Laboratory Hours: Dedicated weekly lab access for senior capstone research groups.
  • Faculty Training Programs: Upskilling professors in generative AI, PyTorch, and cloud deployment.
  • Annual Research Symposium: Co-hosting Mogadishu's premier AI and Emerging Technologies conference.
